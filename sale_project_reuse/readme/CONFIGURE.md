1.  Go to *Sales \> Products \> Products* and open a service product whose
    *Create on Order* is *Project & Task*.
2.  Tick *Reuse Project*, next to *Project Template*.

Optionally, to reuse a project that already exists:

1.  Go to *Sales \> Orders \> Customers* and open the customer company.
2.  In the *Sales & Purchase* tab, set *Reuse Project*.

The field is only shown on the customer company, since the project is shared by
all its contacts.
